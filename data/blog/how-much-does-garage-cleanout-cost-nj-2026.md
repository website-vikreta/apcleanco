## What drives the cost of any garage service?

- Volume, not square footage as most services price by how much space your items take up in the truck, not the size of your garage

- Item types such as appliances, electronics, and paint require separate disposal and can add cost with some companies

- Donation and recycling sorting takes more time but keeps usable items out of landfills (included in AP cleanco's service)

## What a complete quote should always include

Before booking any NJ garage service company, confirm these are in the quoted price:

- All labor, such as loading, carrying, and moving items to the truck

- Hauling and disposal fees, as these should not appear as a surprise on the final bill

- Donation drop-off and recycling sorting if offered

- Whether you pay before, during, or after the job is complete
